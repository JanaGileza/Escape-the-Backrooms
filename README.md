# Escape the Backrooms

Escape the Backrooms is a 2D psychological horror game developed in Unity for a final project I completed in College. Players will have to navigate through this liminal space, collecting key items and avoiding various foes. Will you uncover the truth behind their existence? Will you escape the backrooms?

## Gameplay:

- Explore the backrooms, with ever increasing danger chasing behind you
- Avoid the enemies who linger just outside your vision
- Collect various items, and get to the exit
- Survive.

## Features:

- Original pixel art designed and created using Aseprite
- Multiple endings await your discovery depending on the route you take
- Custom UI & Systems, including flickering lights, easter eggs, and dynamic item counters
- Fully Integrated Scene Flow: Main menu, instructions, win/lose conditions, and credits

## Tools:

- Unity (2D URP)
- C#
- Aseprite
- TextMesh Pro
